## Evaluating the benefits and Risk of Generative AI
**Risk**:
- Regulatory requirements
- Social Risks
- Privacy concerns

**Benefits**:
- Personalization of customer interactions
- Generate novel content
- Easy to adapt prebuilt models to business use cases
- Productivity gains through automation
